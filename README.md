# FNB-SALES-ANALYSIS

This project analyses FNB Sales data to determine the following information;
Daily sales price per unit
Average unit sales price of this product
Daily % gross profit
Daily % gross profit per unit

Determine 3 periods where the product was on promotion/special and answer the below questions
a. What was the Price Elasticity of Demand during each of these periods? 
b. In your opinion, does this product perform better or worse when sold at a 
promotional price? 

Insights are presented on interactive dashboards and an executive presentation to support data-driven decisionsto drive sales growth
